# Geregeld

Geregeld helps solo appointment businesses control when customers can make bookings.

## Language

**Availability**:
The dates and times customers can currently book. It is based on Booking Hours and Schedule Changes and excludes times already occupied by a Booking.
_Avoid_: Bookable hours, booking times

**Booking Hours**:
The recurring times during which a business accepts new online bookings. They are separate from public opening hours and do not change existing bookings.
_Avoid_: Business hours, opening hours

**Schedule Change**:
A temporary change to Booking Hours for one date or a date range. It can close the full day, remove part of a day, or use different hours.
_Avoid_: Date exception, override
